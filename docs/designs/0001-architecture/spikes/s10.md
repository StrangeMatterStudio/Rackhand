# Spike S10: threat list and GeoIP terms

**Question (design 17, S10):** may the default outside sources (Feodo Tracker, Spamhaus DROP, DB-IP Lite) be used by
default in an open-source product, and do they still carry enough entries to be worth shipping as defaults?

**Answer (read and downloaded 2026-11-19):** all three may be used by default. Spamhaus DROP and DB-IP Lite carry plenty
of data. Feodo Tracker is nearly empty: it had 1 address, and its page says its datasets are currently empty. Whether
to keep it as a default is the owner's call. It costs nothing and could fill again.

## Notes from the terms

- **abuse.ch:** Feodo Tracker is "free to use without limitations according to Creative Commons principles (CC0)"
  (terms section 3.4). Section 7.3 of the same page restricts copying abuse.ch's platforms and using their data to
  train AI models; neither applies to a hub that downloads the list and matches addresses against it. The paid
  commercial subscription covers MalwareBazaar, URLhaus and ThreatFox, not Feodo Tracker. So ThreatFox is not a safe
  default replacement.
- **Spamhaus:** EDROP was merged into DROP on 2024-04-10, so DROP alone is the list. The JSON files
  (`drop_v4.json`, `drop_v6.json`, `asndrop.json`) replace the text files, which are being deprecated. Downloads must
  be at least an hour apart or the address may be blocked. The hub's daily refresh fits.
- **DB-IP:** attribution is the text "IP Geolocation by DB-IP" linking to `https://db-ip.com`, on pages that show
  results. That's the Security page and the connection details (7.8.5 already says so).

## Consequences

- 7.8.5's license notes now state these terms. The Security page shows the DB-IP attribution and a Spamhaus credit.
- The hub fetches `drop_v4.json` and `drop_v6.json` (not `drop.txt`), at most daily.
- **Owner decision (only T69 depends on it):** keep Feodo Tracker on by default (recommended: CC0, a tiny daily
  download, and it may fill again), or remove it from the defaults.
